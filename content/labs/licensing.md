From **3 November 2026** the Mayor of London can call in borough licensing decisions on applications of "potential strategic importance" and overturn a refusal or the restrictions a borough has imposed (Licensing Act 2003 as amended, and SI 2026/1078). This project tracks how that power is used.

1. **Cumulative impact zones** — the areas where boroughs presume against new licences. *This map.*
2. **Licensed premises** — every premises licence and club certificate in London, about 36,000 of them. *Councils' registers surveyed; compiling.*
3. **Applications and call-ins** — new licences, variations and reviews, followed from application through any strategic-importance flag and the borough's decision to any call-in. Has the power been used evenly across boroughs, and are applicants now asking for later hours? *Collection of borough notices starts before 3 November.*

No register of flags or call-ins is required by law. Mayor's Questions have been tabled asking for one.
