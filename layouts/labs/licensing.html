London’s cumulative impact zones

Every area where a London licensing authority presumes against new or extended licences because of the combined impact of the premises already there. Compiled from the published licensing policies of all 35 licensing authorities.

About this map

Each zone was taken from the authority’s statement of licensing policy and cumulative impact assessment in force on 16 October 2026, and checked against the Home Office’s count of cumulative impact areas at 31 March 2026 (shown beside each authority). Differences are explained in the authority notes.

Boundaries come from the councils’ own maps and descriptions. Each one carries a precision label:

exactthe council’s own GIS boundary, whole wards, or the whole borough matched to council mapthe council’s PDF map aligned to Ordnance Survey data and the zone traced from it; typically within a few metres traced from descriptionbuilt street by street from the policy’s written boundary approximatethe policy names or hatches streets without exact limits, so the frontages either side are drawn; or only a small-scale map exists

Every boundary was then compared with the council’s own map or description in a separate check, and corrected where they differed.

Unofficial and indicative. This is an independent compilation, not a legal record. Policies change, and a council’s own policy document is the authority on where a zone runs. Check it before relying on a boundary.
