{{- /* bus-stopcost — where along a route the wait from buses cut short lands. One bar per stop, in two parts: the pale part is the wait without the cut-short buses, the strong part on top is what cutting them short added. A tick across each bar marks the timetable's wait. So a reader sees three things at once — what the timetable promised, what ordinary lateness added to it, and what the curtailments added on top — and, above all, WHERE: on the 38 the cost lands almost entirely at Victoria and the first few stops back north, and averaged over the whole route it all but disappears (0.6 min), which is why it is measured stop by stop. Server-rendered like bus-hours.html: the numbers are the point, and every bar carries a native tooltip naming the stop and all three waits, because 50 stops cannot all be labelled. The first and last stops are named under the axis and the hardest-hit one above its bar.
